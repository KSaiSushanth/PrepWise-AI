PROJECT NAME
PrepWise AI

TAGLINE
Your Personal AI Interview Mentor

OBJECTIVE
Build an AI-powered personalized interview preparation platform that helps
students, internship seekers, job seekers, and professionals become
interview-ready through resume analysis, personalized learning, AI tutoring,
mock interviews, and performance feedback.

SUCCESS CRITERIA (V1)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A user should be able to:

1. Create an account.
2. Upload a resume.
3. Select a target company and role.
4. Receive a personalized learning roadmap.
5. Learn topics using the AI Tutor.
6. Take Technical, HR, and Resume-based mock interviews.
7. Receive AI-generated performance and communication feedback.
8. Track interview preparation progress from the dashboard.
